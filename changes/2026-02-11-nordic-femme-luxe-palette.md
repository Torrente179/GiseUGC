# 2026-02-11 - Nordic Femme Luxe Palette

## Summary
Replaced the navy-gold palette with a warm charcoal + dusty rose direction. Navy read as corporate, not modern female content creator. The new palette uses deep warm espresso-charcoal as the primary and a sophisticated dusty rose as the accent — feminine without being pink, luxurious without being loud. Think Charlotte Tilbury, Byredo, Glossier-level taste.

## Changes Made

### Light Mode
1. **Primary** - Warm espresso-charcoal (hue 20, 16% sat) replaces navy. Deep, neutral, chic.
2. **Accent** - Dusty rose (350 24% 60%) replaces burnished gold. The modern feminine signature.
3. **Foreground** - Warm charcoal (20 14% 16%) — zero blue in the palette.
4. **Background** - Warm cream (30 25% 97%) with slightly rosier warmth.
5. **Borders** - Warm toned (hue 28) for palette cohesion.
6. **Focus ring** - Rose-toned to match accent.
7. **Brand teal → sage** - Shifted to soft sage green (160 18% 46%), very Nordic, very modern.
8. **Brand gold → rose gold** - Shifted to warm champagne-rose (15 30% 62%).
9. **Brand cocoa** - Warm taupe-brown (20 14% 30%).
10. **Body gradient** - Updated to warm cream endpoint (30 20% 96%).

### Dark Mode
1. **Primary** - Soft dusty rose (350 22% 72%) as the light-on-dark primary.
2. **Background** - Deep warm dark (20 16% 10%), not cold navy.
3. **Accent** - Muted rose (350 20% 62%) maintained across themes.
4. **All tones warm** — zero cold/blue undertones anywhere.

## Design Philosophy
- Entirely warm palette: charcoal, cream, rose, sage, taupe.
- No navy, no corporate blue tones anywhere.
- Dusty rose accent is restrained (24% saturation) — sophisticated, not bubblegum.
- Nordic structure preserved: clean lines, whitespace, minimalism.
