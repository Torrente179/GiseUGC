# Data Content Model

## Pattern
The route factories do not hard-code most page copy. They call typed data accessors:
- `getServicePageContent(serviceId, locale)`
- `getVerticalPageContent(verticalId, locale)`
- `getResourcePageContent(resourceId, locale)`
- `getLegalPageContent(pageId, locale)`

## Service Pages
`src/data/service-pages.ts` defines each localized service page, including SEO metadata, hero copy, deliverables, best-fit rules, process steps, featured clip ids, FAQs, CTA copy, and related service ids.

## Vertical Pages
`src/data/vertical-pages.ts` follows the same structure for industry verticals such as beauty, fashion, tech/SaaS, ecommerce, and lifestyle/wellness.

## Resource Pages
`src/data/resource-pages.ts` defines editorial article pages. It includes sections, optional comparison tables, FAQs, related service ids, and related vertical ids.

## Legal Pages
`src/data/legal-pages.ts` feeds privacy and content usage pages, including alternates, summary chips, sections, contact details, and effective/update labels.

## Media Catalog Data
`src/data/portfolio-clips.ts` defines the base `ReelClip` shape, R2 URL builders, poster thumb conversion, and legacy clip list. `src/data/nuevos-r2-ready.ts` is generated by the R2 catalog script and extends the runtime catalog.

## Translation Data
`src/locales/en/translation.json` and `src/locales/es/translation.json` drive homepage and navigation copy through i18next. Some static public locale files mirror deployable translation assets under `public/locales/`.
