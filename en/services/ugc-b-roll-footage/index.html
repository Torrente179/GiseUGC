What UGC b-roll is for

What it is

What you can request
Product close-ups, Product in use, Ambient shots, Vertical and horizontal footage
Markets
Brands in the US, Spain, Latin America, and Oceania that edit in-house. B-roll for ads, social posts, landing pages, emails, and product presentations. Often ordered together with lifestyle UGC, ads, or testimonials.
For you if
Your brand has an in-house editing team and needs original footage with a UGC look, not stock. You already work with me on another service and want to add extra shots. Your campaign needs a lot of footage to cut several versions of the same idea.
Not for you if
You need a finished video with voiceover, a script, and a story. My other services cover that. You don’t have anyone to edit and need the video ready to post. You’re looking for one specific shot rather than a package of varied clips.

What you can ask me for

Product close-ups
Close shots of textures, packaging, application, and angles that show off the product.
Product in use
Hands, routines, and moments where you see the product working, no explanation needed.
Ambient shots
Context shots (the space, the light, the objects around the product) that add depth to the final edit.
Vertical and horizontal footage
Depending on the brief, I can deliver both orientations so your team has more options in the edit.

How it works

  1. 1. Product and style brief You tell me about the product, the shots you need, and the look your brand is going for.
  2. 2. Shot list I suggest the types of clips: close-ups, in use, ambient, angles. You approve or adjust.
  3. 3. Filming I film all the shots in natural light, keeping in mind that they need to be easy to edit.
  4. 4. Delivery You get each clip as a separate file, named and organized, ready for your team to cut and combine.
