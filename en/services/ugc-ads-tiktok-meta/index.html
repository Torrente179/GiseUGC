How it works

  1. 1. Angle We pick the angle, the promise, your customer’s main objection, and how well they already know your brand.
  2. 2. Script in parts I split the hook, the middle, the proof, and the CTA so it’s easy to build variations later.
  3. 3. Filming I film so people keep watching and the product is clearly visible, not just so the video looks pretty.
  4. 4. Delivery You get videos ready to upload and test, with alternate hooks so your team can see quickly what works.
