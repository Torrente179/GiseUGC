What a product demo is for

What it is
If your product takes a bit of context to get, seeing it in use helps more than a carousel or a long block of text. I don’t make manuals. I show the product working and explain it the way I would to someone seeing it for the first time.
What you can request
Step-by-step demo, Short tutorial, Technical review with demo, Comparison demo
Markets
Brands in the US, Spain, and Latin America, in Spanish or English. Campaigns for people who know your product exists but don’t yet understand how it works. Educational ads for TikTok and Meta, product pages, and landing pages.
For you if
You run a tech, SaaS, app, or digital service brand and customers need to understand the product before they buy. Your product has a learning curve and a video tutorial clears up a lot of questions. You want educational content in UGC style for ads, landing pages, or onboarding.
Not for you if
Your product makes sense at first glance and needs no explanation. You want a screen recording with nobody on camera. You need a long tutorial or a full course, not a marketing video.

What you can ask me for

Step-by-step demo
I show how the product is actually used, in order and without detours.
Short tutorial
A brief video on one feature, benefit, or use case.
Technical review with demo
Along with showing the product, I explain the technical details that make it credible.
Comparison demo
I show, in use, why your product solves the problem better than the alternative.

How it works

  1. 1. Product and message I get to know what your product does, which benefit to highlight, and who’s going to watch.
  2. 2. Structure I plan the order of the video: quick context, the demo itself, the benefit, and the close. For English videos, I work from your script.
  3. 3. Filming with the product I film using the real product and explain it naturally, keeping the pace up.
  4. 4. Delivery You get videos ready for ads, landing pages, product pages, or social.
