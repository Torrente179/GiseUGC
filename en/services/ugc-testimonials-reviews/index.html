What a video review is for

What it is
A written review helps, but watching someone use the product and talk about it builds more trust. That’s why so many brands put video reviews in their ads and on product pages: they help people make up their minds.
What you can request
Product review, Single-benefit testimonial, Unboxing and first impressions, Comparison review
Markets
Brands in the US, Spain, and Latin America that sell online. Video testimonials for Meta ads, TikTok ads, and retargeting campaigns. Videos for product pages, landing pages, and email marketing.
For you if
You run an ecommerce, beauty, supplement, or lifestyle brand and need video reviews for your product pages. You run paid ads and know trust sells better than a pretty video. Your product has a clear benefit that comes across better when a person talks about it.
Not for you if
You need a tight script with claims that don’t come from real use. Your product doesn’t have a clear benefit someone could describe in first person. You need testimonials from several real customers filmed in different places.

What you can ask me for

Product review
I use the product in my everyday routine and talk about how it went, the way I’d tell a friend.
Single-benefit testimonial
I focus on one specific result: what changed, what it fixed, and why it’s worth it.
Unboxing and first impressions
I open the product on camera and show my reaction. Works as a hook for ads or social posts.
Comparison review
If your product beats an alternative, a side-by-side helps people decide.

How it works

  1. 1. Product and focus You tell me what to highlight, which doubt to address, and the tone you want: casual, excited, or informative.
  2. 2. Key points For Spanish reviews, I put together a short outline so it sounds natural and covers what matters. For English, I work from your script.
  3. 3. Filming with the product I film with the product in hand and actually use it on camera.
  4. 4. Delivery You get the videos 3 to 5 business days after I receive the product and we confirm the brief, ready for ads, social, or your website.
