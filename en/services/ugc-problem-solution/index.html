What the problem-solution format is for

What it is
This format works because it follows how people make decisions: they feel a frustration, recognize the problem, and pay attention when the solution shows up. It isn’t magic, but it’s one of the most common structures in UGC ads.
What you can request
Hook + problem + solution + CTA, Hook variations, Problem-solution with demo, Before and after
Markets
Brands in the US, Spain, and Latin America, in Spanish or English. Conversion and retargeting campaigns on Meta and TikTok. Cold audiences who need to recognize the problem before they’ll look at the product.
For you if
You run an ecommerce or direct-to-consumer brand, already run paid ads, and need ads with a clear structure. Your product fixes a specific problem: it saves time or money, or makes something complicated simple. Your team knows the hook is what makes or breaks an ad.
Not for you if
Your product doesn’t solve a clear problem, or the problem is hard to show on video. You want brand or aspirational content with no direct sales goal. You don’t have a defined offer or a CTA to send people to.

What you can ask me for

Hook + problem + solution + CTA
The classic structure: grab attention, name the problem, introduce your product, and tell people what to do next.
Hook variations
The same video with different openings, so you can test which one stops the scroll in the first 3 seconds.
Problem-solution with demo
For when the solution is easier to get by seeing the product in use.
Before and after

How it works

  1. 1. Problem and audience We find the real problem your ideal customer has: what bothers them, what they’ve tried, and why it’s still not fixed.
  2. 2. Hook and structure For Spanish videos, I write several hook options and lay out the order: problem, solution, proof, and CTA. For English, I work from your script.
  3. 3. Filming I film each part with real emotion and good pacing so people don’t drop off halfway.
  4. 4. Delivery with variations You get videos with different hooks, ready to test, so your team can see quickly which one works.
