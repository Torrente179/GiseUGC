What a spokesperson video is for

What it is
Some offers don’t make sense from an image and a caption. That’s where a person explaining it on camera helps: I lay out the problem, explain what your product solves, and finish with what the viewer should do next.
What you can request
Offer walkthroughs, Videos for your website, Spokesperson ads, Guided demos
Markets
Brands in the US, Spain, and Latin America, in Spanish or English. Launches, promos, service explainers, and demos. Videos you can reuse in ads, on your website, and in outreach to potential customers.
For you if
You have a launch, promo, or service that needs a clear, upfront explanation. Your offer is new or technical and won’t sell on b-roll or on-screen text alone. You want something more structured than casual UGC that still doesn’t feel corporate.
Not for you if
The video is all about the visuals and there’s barely anything to explain. You need complex acting or a heavily performed character. You haven’t settled on the message you want to get across yet.

What you can ask me for

Offer walkthroughs
I explain your product, service, promo, or launch in a short, well-organized video.
Videos for your website
A video for your homepage, a landing page, or a product page that answers the usual questions before someone buys.
Spokesperson ads
Ads where I talk straight to camera, with more structure than casual UGC.
Guided demos
I show step by step how to use your software, service, or product.

How it works

  1. 1. Main message We decide what people need to understand in the first few seconds and which main question the video should answer.
  2. 2. Script We adjust the wording so it sounds like me talking, not someone reading. For English videos, I work from your script, up to 65 words.
  3. 3. Filming I film with attention to diction and pacing so the message lands the first time.
  4. 4. Delivery You get videos ready for ads, your homepage, product pages, or messages to potential customers.
