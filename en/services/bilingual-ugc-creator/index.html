How it works

  1. 1. Brief You tell me who you sell to, what you want to say, and whether you need one language or both.
  2. 2. Script For Spanish, I can write the script or polish yours. For English, I work from your script, up to 65 words per video.
  3. 3. Filming and editing I film and edit each version, making sure the message is easy to follow and the pacing doesn’t drag.
  4. 4. Delivery You get the videos 3 to 5 business days after I receive the product and we confirm the brief. One to two rounds of revisions are included.
