Why use UGC for tech and SaaS

With tech products, the biggest thing stopping a sale is usually confusion. If someone can’t tell what your tool does in the first few seconds, they scroll past. In a UGC video, a real person explains it, shows it working, and ties it to a problem your customer already has. It comes across as a recommendation from someone who’s tried it.
