Why use UGC for beauty

Before someone buys a serum or a foundation, they want to see it on real skin. What’s the texture like? How do you apply it? How does it look afterward? When a real person uses it in their routine and talks about it in their own words, the video comes across as a recommendation from someone who actually uses the product.
